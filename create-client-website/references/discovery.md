# Discovery

Use this checklist to extract client content from a current website, written brief, screenshots, or files.

## Gather

- Business name, tagline, offer, audience, locations, and service area
- Navigation labels, page URLs, page titles, meta descriptions, and headings
- Services, products, industries, process, pricing language, and guarantees
- CTAs, contact details, forms, booking links, social links, and legal links
- Testimonials, case studies, logos, certifications, awards, and partners
- Team members, bios, values, hiring content, and press mentions
- Images, logos, icons, downloads, colors, and recurring visual motifs

## Crawl Strategy

Start with the homepage, then inspect the main navigation, footer links, sitemap, robots hints, and visible service/category pages. Prefer exact client language for claims and offerings.

## Summarize Back

Before building, report the discovered pages, reusable content, missing items, and any risky facts that need confirmation.
